Checklist flow
  1. Dashboard opens first. Schedules in the left menu opens the project cards. Search by number or address, Site Visit opens the report form for that project, and View opens its bookings list
  2. Bookings list: tap Complete on "Client handover" to go straight to the checklist
  3. Or tap the status badge (✎) on any booking to open Update status. Pick a status, add evidence and a note, then tap Submit. On Client handover, choosing Completed continues to the checklist
  4. Checklist: answer the 12 questions in any order. You can Save & exit and come back
  5. Review & submit unlocks when every required question is done
  6. Submit after sign-off, which shows the "Sent for review" confirmation
  7. Close opens the booking detail. A Completion checklist card appears there, and View checklist opens the filled checklist on its own screen
Working
  • Shift (dashboard): Start shift checks the vehicle rego (2BS5GN is expired, so Change vehicle and pick 1AB2CD (okay) or 7XY8ZT (expires in 5 days)) and an odometer at least the last reading. Then Add break (15 / 30 / 60 min, with a countdown you tap to end), Breaks today, and End shift with km driven
  • Every booking has its own checklist. Complete (Schedules or RTS) or choosing Completed in Update status always opens the checklist for that booking
  • Dashboard: Upload and Reschedule (date picker) on today's bookings, View in Upcoming bookings, Add break / End shift, Report a site visit / hazard
  • Every button in the steps above, plus Edit on review rows
  • Yes / Maybe / No, multi-select, follow-up notes and photos, date picker
  • Booking detail: Notes sit under Documents (type, attach, Add). The right panel has the contractor, the Completion checklist (View once submitted) and Activity
  • Site Visits & Hazards (warning icon in the left menu) is its own module: a list of every visit, with + Report site visit (choose the site first). Fill in the request type, questions and photos, then Submit visit to open its detail screen. View opens a visit, and Submitted visits are view-only (no editing); notes can still be added
  • Upload on any booking opens Upload documents. Add photos or files, then Upload. They appear in that booking's Documents
  • Completed bookings (clipboard icon) and RTS Required (flag icon) are their own modules. They use the same booking cards as Schedules. RTS has Complete (moves the booking to Completed Bookings)
  • Tap a booking name in Schedules to open its detail page. The Completion checklist card appears only after the checklist is submitted
  • Status badge ✎ on every booking. Update status needs evidence and a note, and the current status is greyed out
  • Checklist tab filters and the Logs popup on each item
