About me

A little background

I'm a Computer Science and Engineering graduate from Himachal Pradesh Technical University (HPTU), Hamirpur, with a strong interest in Web, UI/UX design and frontend development. I enjoy turning ideas into clean, user-friendly digital experiences.

My work mainly focuses on designing interfaces and user flows in Figma and bringing those designs to life using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, and Tailwind CSS.

I have experience working on CRM dashboards, web applications, responsive websites, and e-commerce platforms using Shopify and WordPress. I also enjoy creating consistent design systems, reusable components, and layouts that balance functionality with visual appeal.

I continuously explore new technologies and AI-powered tools to improve my design and development workflow. I believe in learning, experimenting, and building digital experiences that are both visually engaging and practical.
